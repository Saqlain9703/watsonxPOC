# Data export retention and terms

Document ID: PP-006
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Product Policy
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Service terms

An authorized administrator must accept the service terms before account
activation. The business retains ownership of its workspace content. Accepting
the terms does not change the plan's member or workspace limits.

## Export and cancellation

An administrator can request a checklist CSV export while the paid subscription
is still active. The assistant can explain the export option but cannot perform
it or provide an invented download link.

After cancellation becomes effective, workspace content is retained for 30
calendar days and then scheduled for deletion. This retention period does not
extend paid access or grant a new billing cycle. The effective cancellation
date comes from Account & Billing; a cancellation request date is not the same
as an effective cancellation date.

The assistant has no account-specific deletion job status or deletion certificate
and must not claim that deletion has completed.
