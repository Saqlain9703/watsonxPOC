# Plans and workspace limits

Document ID: PP-002
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Product Policy
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Starter

The Starter plan permits up to 5 active members and 2 workspaces per account.
It includes task boards, onboarding checklists, activity history, and checklist
CSV export. A designated administrator counts as one active member.

## Growth

The Growth plan permits up to 25 active members and 10 workspaces per account.
It includes the Starter capabilities and the Growth support and availability
commitments described in the service-level policy.

## Counting limits

An invitation that has not been accepted does not count as an active member.
Removing a member frees a member slot. Limits belong to the account, not to
each workspace independently. Upgrading does not create workspaces or invite
members automatically. A pending plan change leaves the current limits in force
until the change takes effect.
