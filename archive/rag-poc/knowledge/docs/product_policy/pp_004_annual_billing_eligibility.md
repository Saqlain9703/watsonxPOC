# Eligibility for annual billing

Document ID: PP-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Product Policy
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Eligible plan and status

Annual billing is available only to Growth accounts with active status.
Starter accounts are eligible for monthly billing only. A Starter account must
first complete an upgrade to Growth and have active status before an annual
billing request can be approved.

A Growth account marked past_due or pending_activation is not currently
eligible for an annual billing change. Resolving an outstanding charge does
not itself prove that status has changed; verify the account record again.

## What an eligibility answer means

Meeting these conditions allows an administrator to request annual billing.
It does not mean that a request was submitted, accepted, or scheduled. The
assistant can explain eligibility but cannot approve or execute a change.

The effective date of a confirmed change is governed by Account & Billing's
billing-cycle policy. For a question about a named account, its current plan,
status, and next billing date must come from the account-status tool.
