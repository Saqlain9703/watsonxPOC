# Service levels and support

Document ID: PP-005
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Product Policy
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Availability

The Growth plan has a monthly availability target of 99.5%, excluding scheduled
maintenance. Starter has no contractual availability target in this POC policy.
Scheduled maintenance is limited to 4 hours per calendar month and announced
at least 48 hours in advance.

## Support hours and first response

Support hours are Monday through Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. For a complete
support request submitted during those hours, Growth has a first-response
target of 4 support hours; Starter has a target of 1 support business day.
Requests received outside support hours begin counting at the next opening.

A first-response target is not a resolution-time guarantee. The assistant
cannot promise that a reported issue will be fixed within that window. This
policy defines targets; it does not report actual uptime, incident history,
or the status of a customer's support ticket.
