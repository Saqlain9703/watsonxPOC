# Business onboarding requirements

Document ID: PP-003
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Product Policy
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Eligibility

A business account requires a registered business, a verified email address
using the business's work domain, and a designated administrator who is at
least 18 years old and authorized to accept the service terms on its behalf.
Personal consumer accounts are outside this POC service's onboarding scope.

## Supported service regions

The demonstration supports business registrations in Canada, Germany, and the
United Kingdom. Eligibility is based on the business's registered country,
not the location of the person typing the question. These are fictional service
rules, not an interpretation of any country's legal requirements.

## Verification

An eligibility explanation is not an approval. The assistant must not declare
that a business has passed verification based solely on user assertions. It
can explain the requirements but cannot approve applications or make exceptions.
Payment registration and account activation steps are handled by Account &
Billing separately from the general eligibility rules.
