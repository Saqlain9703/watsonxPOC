# Invoices and billing contacts

Document ID: AB-004
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Billing Operations
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Finding an invoice

A subscription invoice is created at the beginning of each billing cycle.
Administrators can download a PDF copy from Settings > Billing > Invoices.
The billing contact receives an invoice notification. This notification does
not grant access to workspace content.

## Invoice contents

An invoice identifies the subscription plan, billing period, currency, and
amount charged. A displayed invoice is the source for its actual amount and
any tax line. Plan names or account status alone do not establish an invoice
amount or a tax rate.

## Correcting billing details

Changes to the billing contact affect future invoice notifications. Historical
invoice corrections require Billing Support review. The onboarding chat agent
can explain this process but cannot regenerate an invoice or promise a correction.

The account-status tool exposes no invoice number, amount, tax calculation, or
invoice-download link. Do not construct one from a synthetic account ID.
