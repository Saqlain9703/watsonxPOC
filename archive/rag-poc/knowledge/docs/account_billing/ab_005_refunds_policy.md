# Duplicate-charge refunds

Document ID: AB-005
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Billing Operations
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Review and approval

A customer can ask Billing Support to review a suspected duplicate subscription
charge. A request is not an approval. Support must confirm that the two charges
cover the same subscription and period before approving a duplicate-charge refund.

## Processing time

An approved duplicate-charge refund is processed within 7 business days of
approval. Business days for refund processing are Monday through Friday,
excluding Northstar's published business holidays. This document provides no
holiday calendar or account-specific refund status.

## Destination and scope

The refund is returned to the original payment method. It cannot be redirected
to another account. The onboarding agent cannot approve or issue refunds.
No refund approval, amount, or completion date can be inferred from an account's
subscription status. The account-status tool does not contain refund records.
