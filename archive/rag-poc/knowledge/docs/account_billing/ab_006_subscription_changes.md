# Subscription changes and billing handbook

Document ID: AB-006
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Billing Operations
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Plan changes

An administrator requests a plan change in Settings > Billing > Subscription.
A request requires confirmation before it is scheduled. Changes take effect at
the next renewal. The current period keeps its existing plan and limits.
Eligibility for the requested plan or billing frequency comes from Product &
Policy; an account lookup alone does not grant that eligibility.

## Cancellation

Confirmed cancellation takes effect at the end of the current paid period.
Access continues until that point. An account whose cancellation is effective
has no next billing date. A chat response cannot cancel an account.

## Duplicate-charge refund handling

An approved duplicate-charge refund is processed within 14 business days of
approval and is returned to the original payment method. A pending refund
request is not an approved refund. Support confirms approval separately.

This handbook and other current Billing Operations policies have equal
authority. No precedence or supersession rule is defined between them.
