# Account setup and activation

Document ID: AB-001
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-09-01
Owner: Account Operations
Classification: Synthetic POC policy for the fictional Northstar Workspace service.

## Creating an account

An authorized administrator creates a workspace using a verified work email
address, selects a plan, and supplies a billing contact. A billing contact can
receive invoices without becoming a workspace member. Do not collect card
numbers, passwords, or identity documents in a chat conversation.

The account remains pending activation until the work email is verified, the
required terms are accepted, and a supported payment method is registered.
Creating an account alone does not activate it or begin a billing cycle.

## Invitations

A workspace invitation expires 48 hours after it is issued. After expiry, an
administrator must issue a new invitation. Resending does not activate the
account or prove that the recipient has accepted the invitation.

## Account-specific information

Account identifiers in this demonstration have the format ACC followed by a
hyphen and four digits, such as ACC-0001. These identifiers are synthetic.
The account-status lookup is the source for a particular account's status,
plan, billing frequency, and next billing date. This document contains general
setup rules, not individual customer records.
